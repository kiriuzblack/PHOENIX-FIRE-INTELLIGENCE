These NASA resources provide experimental data, scientific background, historical safety cases, and technology-transfer examples.

- [NASA PSI Combustion Data](https://psi.nasa.gov/physci/repo/search?q=combustion)
- [NASA Combustion Science](https://www.nasa.gov/glenn/glenn-expertise-space-exploration/physical-sciences-program/combustion-science/)
- [NASA ScienceCasts: Combustion on the ISS](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=W1oXykoz9zg)
- [ISS Combustion Science Researcher's Guide](https://www.nasa.gov/missions/station/a-researchers-guide-to-combustion-science/)
- [Apollo 1](https://sma.nasa.gov/sma-disciplines/accllp/apollo-1)
- [Apollo 13](https://sma.nasa.gov/sma-disciplines/accllp/apollo-13)
- [1997 Mir Space Station Fire](https://www.nasa.gov/history/25-years-ago-fire-aboard-space-station-mir/)
- [Fireman's Air Tanks](https://spinoff.nasa.gov/node/9582)
- [Fire Detector](https://spinoff.nasa.gov/node/9407)
- [New Technology for the Fire Attire](https://spinoff.nasa.gov/node/9918)

## Evidence Categories

- **Experimental evidence:** PSI FLEX and SAFFIRE data.
- **Historical cases:** Apollo 1, Apollo 13, and Mir.
- **Context and technology:** NASA research guides, videos, and Spinoff projects.

Historical cases and technology examples are not included in the 285 experimental tests.

These references do not imply NASA endorsement.
